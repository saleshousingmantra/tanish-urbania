By Tanish Group · Published 26 November 2026 · Updated 26 November 2026

Tanish Urbania is a contemporary residential community in Charholi, in the PCMC area of Pune. It is also searched as Tanish Urbania Charholi or Tanish Urbania PCMC, and this page brings the main facts together.

Design and planning

The twelve wings are positioned to give open space, daylight and cross ventilation. The architecture is defined by clean geometry, vertical accents and balanced proportions, with generous balconies and a subtle play of colour. Landscaped zones are spread through the master plan so that residents have open areas that bring people together.

Location

Charholi is one of the faster-developing pockets of Pimpri-Chinchwad, with new infrastructure and landmark projects emerging around it. Read the Charholi locality overview and the guide to roads, metro, rail and airport, or check live routes on Google Maps.
