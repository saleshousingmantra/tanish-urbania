By Tanish Group · Published 26 November 2026 · Updated 26 November 2026

Tanish Urbania offers two home types in Charholi, PCMC: 2 BHK and 3 BHK. Here is how they compare and what to ask the sales team before you decide.

Areas are approximate. Pricing, floor plans and the brochure are shared on enquiry.

Which one suits you

A 2 BHK suits couples, small families and first-time buyers who want an efficient layout. A 3 BHK suits families who want an extra bedroom for children, guests or a home office. For a fuller comparison, read 2 BHK or 3 BHK: how to choose the right home.

Questions to ask before you choose

  1. Is the stated size carpet area or built-up area?
  2. Which wings and floors have each home type, and what are the views?
  3. What is the total package, and what does it include?
  4. What is the payment schedule, and what is the RERA registration number?
